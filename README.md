Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Everyday Things, Two Lines, Longer Run"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-everyday-things-two-lines-longer-run-f8734479).

`main.ts` (Node, stdlib only) writes a two-line explanation to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`:

- **Line 1** explains one everyday thing — a queue — to a 10-year-old in a single vivid sentence built on one analogy: a queue is a human train.
- **Line 2** says in one sentence why the analogy works: what it maps (everyone moving together in order, waiting their turn) and what it leaves out (people can step out, chat, or cut in — carriages never do).

The program also guards the gate before writing: exactly two non-empty lines, each a single sentence, 1–400 characters total.
